# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A two-line warning sent back to my past self, written for [make.fun's Two Lines to Your Past Self bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca).

Line one warns my past self about one specific moment — the morning Dad offered the keys to the old motorbike. Line two traces what that moment cost: the brakes failing three weeks later, a month in a cast, and the scholarship interview lost.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node stdlib only, no network, no clock, no randomness) validates the entry — exactly two non-empty lines with no leading/trailing whitespace and no trailing newline, 1–280 characters — writes it to `entry.txt` under `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, then verifies the written file round-trips byte-identical.
